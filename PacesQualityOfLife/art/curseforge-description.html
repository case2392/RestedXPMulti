Pace's Quality of Life

Small conveniences for World of Warcraft Forever's own windows. Each one can be switched off in Options > AddOns > Pace's Quality of Life, or with /pqol.

Quest log: track or untrack a whole zone

  • Every zone header in the Map & Quest Log (Redridge Mountains, Ashenvale, Blackfathom Deeps...) gets a tracking checkbox like the ones on the quests. Click it to track every quest in that zone, click again to untrack them all. A faint tick means some of the zone is tracked.
  • The Quests: 34/40 box above the list has one for all quests, and the list's gear menu has Track all quests and Untrack all quests.
  • The game tracks at most 25 quests at once, so "track all" fills up to 25, the zones on the open map first, and says how many didn't fit. A box that has filled what it can counts as full: its next click untracks.

Looking For Group: players by role

  • Three role buttons (tank, healer, damage) sit between Send Message and Group Invite. One click on the healer shows only healers. Click others to add or remove roles; clear the last one to see everyone again.
  • While a filter is on, the matching players show in a list of their own with its own Send Message and Group Invite buttons. Select a player, then click; a double-click whispers.
  • Blizzard's own list is never changed. Inside dungeons, raids and encounters the game hides this data from add-ons, so the filter steps aside there and Blizzard's list shows as usual.

Objective tracker follows your zone

  • When you enter a zone, the tracker switches to that zone's quests: everything under the zone's quest log header is tracked, the rest untracked. Go to Duskwood and you see Duskwood's quests.
  • It happens once per zone: anything you track or untrack by hand stays until you enter the next zone (a /reload doesn't count).
  • A dungeon's quests show inside the dungeon; the quest your arrow points to stays tracked; a zone with none of your quests (a city) leaves the tracker as it was; nothing changes during combat or a flight (it catches up after).

Chat Channels: who's in voice, before you join

  • Forever's Chat Channels window only shows who is in a community's voice channel once you're in it. With Pace's QoL, members in the voice channel get a microphone next to their name before you join.
  • The game doesn't tell add-ons who's in a voice channel you haven't joined, so Pace's QoL users tell each other: opening Chat Channels on a community asks the members an add-on can reach - your Battle.net friends playing Forever, your guild and your group, and in a WoW community the members of your faction - and the ones in voice who also run Pace's QoL answer, and say when they join or leave. Ask your friends to install it.
  • These are invisible add-on messages: nothing appears in chat, and nothing is sent in dungeons or encounters (a join or leave made there goes out once you're out). You can stop answering in the options.
  • A microphone missing? /pqol voice on both sides says what was sent, what came back and what the game refused (no names).
  • A channel you joined earlier this session and left with the headset shows everyone in it, add-on or not: the game still knows who's there.

Voice chat: each person's volume, kept

  • Right-click someone in voice (or their party frame, in party voice) and the slider sets how loud they are - but the game doesn't keep it. Pace's QoL remembers the volume you set for each person and sets it again whenever they're in voice with you: when they join, when you join, after a /reload and the next time you play.
  • One volume per person: a Battle.net friend in a Battle.net community with you, set from their party frame and from the community's roster, gets the one you set last.
  • Only what you set with the slider is kept, never the game's own resets. In dungeons and raids nothing is set (the game's chat lockdown); a change you make there is kept once you're out, not undone - except a change to 100% from a member list opened in there: everyone starts at 100%, so whose it was can't be told. /pqol voicevolume forget forgets them all.

Party frames: your party's pets

  • The standard party frames have a pet frame under each member, but WoW Forever's options have no switch for it. Tick "Party frames: show your party members' pets" and your party's pets show - after your next /reload (the options page has a Reload now button), so the party frames take it cleanly and keep working in combat.
  • It is the game's own setting, so it stays as you left it. Untick to hide them again. Each character's switch is its own. If the game refuses the switch, you're told what to type instead. For the raid-style party frames, use their own pet option.

Commands

  • /pqol - the options page
  • /pqol status - what's on, and any errors this session
  • /pqol questlog|lfgroles|zonetracker|voice|voicevolume|partypets on|off - switch a feature
  • /pqol voice - what voice sharing sent, heard and was refused (/pqol voice log: the last messages)
  • /pqol voicevolume forget - forget every volume kept
